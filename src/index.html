06 / A few answers

Before you connect.

How do I get a game server?

Email us with the game and edition, expected player count, and any mods or plugins. We’ll discuss availability, requirements and pricing with you.

Can I bring an existing world or mods?

Include your game version, mod list and what you’d like to migrate in your enquiry. Compatibility and migration requirements need to be checked for your specific setup.

Where do I manage my server?

Existing users can sign in at play.frostbyte.it (opens in a new tab). If you need an account or help accessing an instance, contact admin@frostbyte.it.

Do you help with projects beyond gaming?

Yes. Get in touch about server maintenance, backups, automation, bots or dashboards. We’ll work through the scope and what’s possible for your project.

How much does it cost?

There’s no fixed package listed here. Cost depends on resources, game requirements and the scope of the work. Ask for a quote before deciding.
